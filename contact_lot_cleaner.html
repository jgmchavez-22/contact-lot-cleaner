Contact Lot Cleaner

Upload a contact lot, set your targets, and download a dialer-ready, cleaned spreadsheet. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data leaves your machine.

1 · Contact lot file

Excel (.xlsx/.xls) or CSV.

Drag & drop your file here, or click to browse
Tip: if nothing appears after you pick a file, make sure it isn't open in Excel, then try again.

2 · Target country / countries

Comma-separated. Numbers outside these are dropped (kept & flagged only if it's the contact's sole number).

3 · Excluded job titles

One title/keyword per line. A Job Title containing any of these is tagged DNC – Not Target Job Title (case-insensitive).

4 · Run

Builds Upload/Result Code, splits names, cleans phones to digits-with-country-code, adds Region, screens titles, sorts, and re-orders columns.
